# Review build

Two things are in this repo at once:

1. **Fixes that are already applied** — corrections with no design judgement in
   them, listed under *Applied directly* below. These are live now.
2. **Two competing designs**, both coded, both switched by a `<body>` class:
   - **Wave one** (`p01`…`p18`) — individual improvements to the current design.
   - **Wave two** (`inst`…) — one coherent institutional design system.

Wave one is what is committed today. Wave two is the proposal.

## Seeing it

```
python -m http.server 8000
```

Then <http://127.0.0.1:8000>. A **Review** panel appears bottom-left with a
checkbox per item. It remembers your choices as you move between pages, and the
page links at the bottom of it walk you through the whole site.

The two waves are mutually exclusive — they answer the same questions
differently — so ticking anything in one group clears the other. **Reset** goes
back to what is committed.

`DEV_MODE` at the top of `scripts/proposals.js` is `false` — the deployed
setting. With it off the file returns immediately and renders nothing, so the
panel will not appear. **Set it back to `true` to review, and to `false` again
before deploying.**

## Choosing

The `<body>` class in the HTML is the single source of truth. Copy the string
from the panel onto `<body>` in `index.html`, `blog.html`, `article.html` and
the four policy pages to keep a set.

To adopt wave two:

```html
<body class="inst inst-hero inst-process inst-team inst-services inst-results inst-faq inst-footer">
```

`inst-stats` and `p06` are gone. Both drove the credibility band under the hero,
and the band was dropped in favour of the existing `diffgraph` chart, so the
markup they styled no longer exists.

### Removed by decision, not resolved

The registered entity name, company number and registered office address were
taken off all four policy pages on 24 August 2026 at your instruction, along
with the postal line in every contact block.

Be aware of what that means. A UK-registered company has to give its registered
name, its company number and its registered office address on its website —
that is the Companies Act 2006 trading-disclosure rules and the E-Commerce
Regulations 2002, and it is not optional. UK GDPR separately requires a privacy
notice to identify the controller, which is what the "Who we are" block existed
to do. These pages are non-compliant on that point until the details go back in.

## Known limitations

- **The header and footer are duplicated across eight HTML files.** That was
  already true of three; the policy pages made it worse. Either accept it and
  edit all eight together, or add a small build step. It is the main thing
  standing between this repo and being comfortable to maintain.
- **Google Fonts is a third-party request** on every page. Self-hosting the five
  Merriweather weights removes it, is faster, and removes the only item in
  `cookies.html` that needs explaining.
- **No enquiry form and no booking link.** Both CTAs open a dialog that asks
  people to send a WhatsApp message or an email. A form endpoint and a
  scheduling link would convert considerably better.
- **No analytics**, so none of the above can be measured. If you add any, the
  cookie policy needs a cookie table and, for UK/EU visitors, a consent banner
  that blocks the script until they agree.
