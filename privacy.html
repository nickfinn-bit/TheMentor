Who we are

TheUniMentor provides university admissions consulting, academic tutoring and entrance examination preparation.

Needed from you

The registered legal entity name, its jurisdiction of incorporation, its company registration number and its registered office address. Where the business is established determines which data protection law applies — UK GDPR, EU GDPR, a US state privacy law, the Hong Kong PDPO, or more than one at once — and that in turn changes the wording of several sections below.

The site currently states that management is in the United States while describing a student base in the UK and Hong Kong. That combination usually means more than one regime applies.

What we collect

We collect only what we need in order to answer an enquiry and deliver a service:

Enquiry information
The name, contact details, year group, subjects and academic aspirations you send us when you request a consultation, by email or through WhatsApp.
Engagement information
For students we work with: target courses and universities, predicted and achieved grades, drafts of written application material, session notes and progress updates.
Parent or guardian information
Name and contact details, where a parent or guardian is the person arranging or paying for the engagement.

Website information
This site does not run analytics and sets no cookies of its own. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google and Google receives your IP address. See our Cookie Policy.

Why we use it, and on what basis

  • To respond to enquiries and arrange consultations. Because you asked us to, and because it is in our legitimate interest to answer people who contact us.
  • To deliver the service you have engaged us for. To perform our contract with you.
  • To keep records of sessions and progress. Our legitimate interest in running the service properly, and yours in being able to see what has been done.
  • To take payment and keep accounting records. To perform our contract, and to comply with legal obligations.
  • To send occasional updates about our services. Only with your consent, and you can withdraw it at any time.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it to build advertising profiles.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only where it is necessary:

  • With the consultant or tutor assigned to a student, so that they can prepare and deliver sessions.
  • With service providers who process information on our behalf — [list them: email provider, video conferencing, scheduling, payments, file storage] — under written terms that require them to keep it confidential and use it only for us.
  • Where we are required to do so by law, or where there is a safeguarding concern; see our Safeguarding Statement.

We do not share a student's information with a university except at your direction.

International transfers

Our students, our consultants and our service providers are in different countries, so personal information may be transferred across borders.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not become engagements: [e.g. 12 months] from the last contact.
  • Engagement records, including session notes and drafts: [e.g. 3 years] after the engagement ends, so that we can answer questions about work we did.
  • Financial records: for as long as tax and company law requires [state the period for the relevant jurisdiction].

We delete or anonymise information once these periods have passed.

Students under 18

Most of the students we work with are under 18. Where that is the case we expect a parent or guardian to arrange the engagement, and we will provide progress updates to that parent or guardian.

We do not knowingly collect information directly from a child without a parent or guardian being aware of the engagement. If you believe we hold information about a child that we should not, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the information we hold about you;
  • correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete information we no longer need;
  • restrict or object to how we use it;
  • transfer it to another provider in a portable format;
  • stop sending you marketing, at any time.

To exercise any of these, contact us using the details below. We will respond within one month. If you are not satisfied with our response you can complain to the relevant supervisory authority [name the authority for the jurisdiction — for the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk].
